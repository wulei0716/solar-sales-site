Common questions

1688 Agent FAQ

What is a 1688 agent?

A China-based coordinator who can communicate with suppliers, place orders, receive goods, check them, consolidate shipments, and arrange export delivery.

What is your service MOQ?

There is no single product MOQ from us, but supplier MOQs, order value, SKU count, and shipping efficiency all affect whether a project is practical.

How do I pay for 1688 orders?

After the order plan and costs are confirmed, you pay the agreed amount to the agent, who handles supplier settlement in China and records the transaction.

How do I choose a reliable agent?

Look for clear fee rules, written order records, responsive communication, receiving evidence, shipment measurements, and a process for handling shortages or visible defects.

Do bulk orders receive a lower rate?

The pricing table uses lower percentage rates as order value increases. Complex customization or inspections can still require separate fees.

How long does collection take?

Many domestic deliveries arrive within several days, but the full collection time depends on each supplier’s stock, production lead time, and location.

Can you act as exporter of record?

Export arrangements depend on the product category, destination, customs requirements, and shipping method. Confirm this during the quotation stage.

Can you provide shipping documents?

Commercial invoices, packing lists, and other standard shipment documents can be coordinated when required and legally applicable.

Can shipping be estimated before purchase?

Yes, using product dimensions, quantity, packaging assumptions, and past experience. The final charge is confirmed after the goods are received and measured.
